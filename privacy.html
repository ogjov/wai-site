Privacy policy

Last updated October 11, 2026

What WAI accesses

When you connect Google Calendar, WAI requests access to your calendar so it can show your events and create, update or delete events when you ask it to. WAI does not access your Gmail, contacts, files or any other Google data.

How your data is used

  • Calendar data is used only to display and manage your own events inside WAI.
  • When you ask the assistant a calendar question, the relevant event titles and times may be sent to an AI model provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) solely to answer that request.
  • Your data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for advertising.

Storage

Access tokens and a short-lived cache of your events are stored on the private server that runs WAI. They are not shared with anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

WAI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can disconnect Google Calendar from inside WAI at any time, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting deletes the stored tokens and cached events.
